December Reading Mission

Two minutes of reading. A little speaking. Five sight words. Every day.

Small steps, strong habits

Our Focus

Children often understand more than they can express aloud. Short, positive conversations help them share their ideas with confidence. Our goal is to make English speaking practice consistent, natural, and enjoyable—not to add more pressure.

1. Read & Retell

2 minutes of reading aloud daily + a short voice recording

  • Read a book or passage aloud for at least two minutes.
  • Record a short retelling: What was it about?
  • Say what you liked or didn't like—and why.
  • Use one detail from the reading to support your opinion.
Sentence starters
“This story was about…”
“I liked / didn't like… because…”
“One part that shows this is…”

2. Five Sight Words

Make recognition quick and fun.

  • Practice five familiar or new sight words daily.
  • Try a word hunt, matching cards, a speed round, or a silly sentence game.
  • Repeat the same words until recognition becomes automatic; then swap in one or two new words.

3. Talk About Thinking

Ask one friendly follow-up question after the retelling:

“Why do you think that?”
“What makes you say that?”
“Can you give me an example?”

Let your child finish speaking before correcting small grammar mistakes. Celebrate thoughtful ideas.
