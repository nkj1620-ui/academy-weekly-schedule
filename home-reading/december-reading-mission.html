December Reading & Sight Word Mission

Read for two minutes, retell your ideas, and enjoy five sight words a day.

Our December Goal

Build confident oral expression through small, joyful daily routines. Deep thinking deserves opportunities to be heard. Focus on sharing ideas clearly, not perfect grammar or long assignments.

1 · Read & Retell

Read aloud for 2 minutes every day. Then record a short voice retelling with a parent’s help.

  1. What was the story about?
  2. What did you like or dislike?
  3. What detail from the story supports your opinion?
Try saying:

“This story was about…”
“I liked / didn't like… because…”
“One part that shows this is…”

Recordings are made using your family's device and can be shared directly with the teacher if requested. This webpage does not collect audio.

2 · Talk About Thinking

Parents, ask one friendly follow-up question:

“Why do you think that?”
“What makes you say that?”
“Can you give an example from the story?”

Listen first and encourage complete thoughts. Avoid interrupting to correct every small grammar error.

3 · Sight Word Practice

Choose your level below. Start with First Grade and practice five words at a time. Repeat words until they feel easy before moving on. The word bank follows the Dolch Sight Word Book shared by Raquel.

Game 1 · Word Flip

Look, say the word aloud, then flip to see a short sentence.

Game 2 · Find the Word

Read the target word and tap the matching word. Say it aloud before tapping!

Game 3 · Beat Your Time

Read all five words aloud. A family member can time you. Try again for smoother reading, not just speed.

More Games with Mom

Adapted from 21 Dolch Sight Word Activities (MrsPerkins.com). Pick just one each day—five minutes is enough.

Apple Picking · Activity #21

Tap an apple, read the hidden sight word aloud, and collect it! Mom can ask you to use one word in a sentence.

Silly Sentences · Activity #3

Pick a sight word. Say a funny sentence using it. The sillier, the better! Mom can write it down or record your voice.

Prompt: “Can you tell me a funny sentence with this word?”

Snatch a Card · Activity #20

Off-screen game: Write today's five words on small paper cards. Place them face down, mix them up, and take turns grabbing one. Read it correctly to keep it. If it's tricky, Mom reads it with you and puts it back. No special supplies needed!
