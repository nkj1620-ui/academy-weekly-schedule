Reading comprehension & visual analysis

1. Why do people celebrate?

Model answer

The Shah has left, and many people see his departure as a victory.

2. How does Satrapi convey a shared mood?

Model answer

Many people appear together in celebration. A reader might point to repeated gestures and explain how they suggest shared excitement.

3. What does the teacher ask the students to do?

Model answer

She tells them to remove the Shah’s picture from their books.

4. Why does Marji challenge this instruction?

Model answer

She remembers the teacher once presented the Shah’s rule as divinely authorized.

5. Why do the children single out Ramin?

Model answer

They hear that his father killed people for the Shah’s secret police and direct their anger at his son.

6. What mistake do they make about responsibility?

Model answer

They treat a child as answerable for a parent’s actions.

7. How does Marji’s mother intervene?

Model answer

She stops Marji and challenges the idea that Ramin deserves punishment for what his father did.

8. What is complicated about Marji’s offer of forgiveness?

Model answer

Her intention has changed, yet offering forgiveness still suggests that Ramin himself needs to be forgiven.

9. How does Ramin respond?

Model answer

He defends his father’s actions. His reply shows that the children have heard very different accounts at home.

10. Why return to the party scene after reading the ending?

Model answer

The crowd’s shared joy contrasts with a disagreement between two children that political change has not settled.
