0–5 MIN · OPEN YOUR BOOK TO PAGE 92

Choose a panel you remember

Show your teacher one panel from your school reading. Who is there? What just happened?

Which detail helped you remember this moment?

“I remember the panel where ____. The detail that stayed with me was ____.”

Need a quick recap?

Earlier, Marji learned about revolution through stories of imprisonment in her own family. Even after the Shah fell, people close to her were still in danger. Now Iran is at war with Iraq. Mali and her family have lost their home in the south and are staying with Marji’s family.

5–10 MIN · THE JEWELS · PP. 92–93

Listen from Mali’s side

Read the last exchange in the shop. Find the women who are speaking, then find Mali. What do the speakers assume about people arriving from the south?

Why would hearing those words hurt Mali, especially after losing her home?

Point to one speech bubble or facial expression. Try “The women assume ____. From Mali’s point of view, ____.”

10–25 MIN · THE KEY · BEGINS ON P. 94

One key, two meanings

Read from the chapter opening to the conversation in which Mrs. Nasrine explains what her son received at school. Pause there.

  1. Find the promise. What has the boy been told the key will open? What must happen first?
  2. Read his mother’s reaction. Pick one detail in her face, posture, or words. What does she fear?
  3. Interpret the symbol. Why might giving a boy an actual object make the promise feel more real?
“At school, the key stands for ____. To his mother, it means ____ because ____.”
Word help

Paradise means heaven in the promise made to the boys. A symbol is an object or image that stands for an idea. To persuade someone is to try to change what they believe or do.

25–30 MIN · EXIT TICKET

Put the families side by side

What does Mali’s family lose, and what is Mrs. Nasrine afraid of losing?

Answer aloud in two or three sentences. Name one panel and describe one thing you can actually see or read in it.

“War affects Mali’s family by ____. Mrs. Nasrine fears ____. In the panel where ____, I notice ____, which suggests ____.”

교사용 진행 메모 · 예상 답변과 속도 조절

첫 5분 윤재가 누가 무엇을 겪었는지 자기 말로 설명하면 바로 가게 장면으로 이동하세요. 사건이 섞이면 위의 ‘Need a quick recap?’을 함께 읽고 “Why is Mali staying with Marji?” 한 가지만 확인하세요.

The Jewels 말리는 집을 잃은 뒤 같은 나라 사람들에게 남부 피란민을 향한 모욕까지 듣습니다. 윤재가 “She feels sad”라고 하면 “What did she hear that made it worse?”로 구체화해 주세요.

The Key 소년은 전쟁에서 죽으면 열쇠로 천국에 들어갈 수 있다는 말을 듣습니다. 어머니에게 그 열쇠는 아들을 잃을 수 있다는 위협으로 보입니다. 윤재가 소년의 마음을 단정하면 “Which words tell us what he was told? Which part are you inferring?”라고 물어보세요.

시간이 부족하면 열쇠가 처음 소개되는 장면까지만 읽고 “What would you ask Mrs. Nasrine about the key?”로 마무리하세요. 다음 시간에는 윤재가 고른 질문으로 이어가면 됩니다.

마무리 답변 예시 “Mali has already lost her home, and in the shop she hears other people judging families like hers. Mrs. Nasrine is afraid she could lose her son. When she shows the key, an ordinary object carries that fear.” 윤재가 책에서 고른 근거가 다르면 그 컷을 중심으로 들어 주세요.

쪽수가 다른 판본에서는 The Jewels의 마지막 가게 장면과 The Key의 나스린 아주머니 대화를 기준으로 찾으세요.
