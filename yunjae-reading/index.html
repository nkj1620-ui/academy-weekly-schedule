1

Why does Nya doubt that water can be found?

예시 답안

The ground looks dry and hard. Her conclusion comes from what she can see on the surface.

2

What work takes place in Chapter 11?

예시 답안

Villagers clear trees, bushes, and grass between the trees. Snakes and scorpions make the task dangerous.

3

How do Marial and Uncle continue to influence Salva?

예시 답안

He believes they would want him to survive. Remembering them gives him determination.

4

How does the group treat Salva after Uncle dies?

예시 답안

They complain about him and give him little food or companionship. He wants to prove their judgment wrong.

5

Why is camp life both better and difficult?

예시 답안

He has food and protection from the war, but is separated from familiar people and cannot find his family.

6

What is the iron giraffe?

예시 답안

It is the tall drill. The metaphor connects unfamiliar equipment with an animal Nya knows.

7

How do the villagers help the drilling project?

예시 답안

Women carry rocks and other villagers pound them into gravel. The work involves many people.

8

Does Chapter 12 confirm the fate of every family member?

예시 답안

No. Salva concludes that his family is gone. Distinguish his belief from independently verified information.

9

How does Salva adapt Uncle’s advice?

예시 답안

He focuses on surviving the rest of one day, just as he once focused on reaching a nearby landmark.

10

What changes in July 1991, and what danger follows?

예시 답안

Soldiers force the refugees out of Ethiopia toward the Gilo River. The current is swollen by rain and crocodiles live there.

Why does Nya doubt the visitors' claim?

What is Uncle's practical strategy in the desert?

He divides the journey into nearby targets. A small, visible goal can reduce the feeling of being overwhelmed.

What might using Salva's full name accomplish?

예시 답안 / Teacher notes

It connects him to his identity, family, and home. This is an interpretation supported by his memories in Chapter 9.

Why does Uncle stop Salva from sharing his water?

예시 답안 / Teacher notes

He judges that Salva is too young and weak to survive without it. This is protection, rather than proof that helping is wrong.

What competing responsibilities do the women face?

예시 답안 / Teacher notes

Does Uncle confirm that Salva's family is dead?
