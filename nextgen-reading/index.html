Middle School Must-Read Library

Grades 6–8 · Four seasons · Twelve anchor books · One growing reading curriculum

Our reading pathway

Read deeply. Think critically. Write clearly.

Each grade has four anchor texts, one per season. We will add chapter-by-chapter lessons, academic vocabulary, discussion prompts, evidence-based writing, and teacher answer keys over time.

G6: character, plot, inference & theme · G7: symbolism, perspective & literary essays · G8: complex themes, rhetoric & comparative analysis.

Current status: This is a curriculum roadmap. The 12 book lesson packages are not yet published. Text difficulty should be assigned after checking the exact edition and learners, rather than presenting unverified Lexile numbers; links will appear as units become available.

Reusable unit template

What students will find in every unit

01 · Before ReadingBackground, essential question, prediction and key terms
02 · During ReadingChapter slides, vocabulary, annotation and text-dependent questions
03 · After ReadingDiscussion, theme/character analysis, compare and contrast
04 · WritingParagraph or essay prompt, rubric, revision checklist and model answers

How we build this library

Start with one pilot unit per grade; reuse its layout to publish later chapters. Keep remaining must-read titles as independent reading recommendations and expand the catalog after the anchor units are established.

Teacher note: Some G7–G8 titles include war, racism, violence or other mature themes. Select and pace with student readiness in mind. Avoid uploading copyrighted book text or identifying student information to public pages.
